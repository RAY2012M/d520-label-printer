    Bluetooth isn't available in this browser. On iPhone, Safari can't talk to Bluetooth printers. Install the free Bluefy – Web BLE Browser app from the App Store and open this same page inside Bluefy. Turn the Labelife/other printer app off first, since the printer accepts one phone at a time.
